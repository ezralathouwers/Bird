Changes: selected primary habitat attributes only, matched scientific names
and existing scientific aliases to stable app IDs, translated habitat classes
into reusable visual scene IDs. No source images or narrative descriptions
are reproduced. No endorsement by the authors is implied.
Source-derived entries are automatic exact-name/alias mappings, not individual
expert reviews. Taxonomically unresolved species remain unassigned.
The ten individually checked pilot assignments retain their separate sources.
